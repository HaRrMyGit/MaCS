$X_{/G}$ - мн-во всех орбит
$\varphi g(x) = gx$
$x^g = \{x \in X|gx = x\}$
===Теорема:===
$|X_{/G}| = \frac{1}{|G|}\cdot\sum_{g \in G}{|x^g|}$
Док-во:
	$F =\{(g, x)|gx = x\} \subset G \times X$      $F = \sqcup_{g \in G}{x^g} = \cup_{x \in X}{Stabx}$
	$\sum{|x^g|} = \sum_{x \in X}{|Stabx|} = \sum{\frac{|G|}{|Orbx|}} = |G| \sum{\frac{1}{|Orbx|}}$ =
	= $|G|\sum_{\alpha \in X_{/G}}{\frac{1}{|Orbx|}} = |G|\sum_{\alpha \in X_{/G}}{1} = |G||X_{/G}|$

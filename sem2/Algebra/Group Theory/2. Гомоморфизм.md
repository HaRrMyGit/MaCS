1. $f(e) = e_H$
2. $f(g^{-1}) = (f(g))^{-1}$

1. $f(e) = f(e \cdot e) = f(e) \cdot f(e)$     |$(f(e))^{-1} \ \ => e_H = f(e_G)$
2. $e = f(e) = f(g\cdot g^{-1}) = f(g) \cdot f(g^{-1})$    |$\cdot (f(g))^{-1}$
   $(f(g))^{-1} = f(g^{-1})$
